Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Find quick answers to common questions about our gown rental, booking fittings, and policies.

We offer HMUA (hair and makeup) starting at P600 only. This package is ideal for simple and elegant looks suitable for various occasions such as photoshoots, events, and special celebrations.

We do not accept on-site bookings at your location. Clients are required to come to us instead, as we are a home-based service.

If we travel to your location, the service fee will be higher and will depend on negotiation, since it is more convenient and involves additional time and effort for HMUA services.

By coming to our place, you can also enjoy our standard rates without additional charges. We assure you that we will provide you with quality service at the best possible price when you visit us directly.

For convenience and proper scheduling, please message us first before coming.

We have a promo sale until August 10 where all gowns, accessories, and heels are only 500!

We also offer a bundle promo for 1,000 (previously 1,300-1,500) which includes hair and makeup, gown rental, heels/sandals, and accessories. Please note that hair and makeup services are for walk-ins at our shop only; we do not travel to customer locations. Exceptions can be negotiated, but the price will increase.

Free gown adjustments are included!

To reserve a gown, we require a security deposit ranging from 300 to P500, depending on the gown and selected bundle, along with a valid ID. This serves as confirmation of your reservation.

Our gown rentals may include free use of accessories or heels, subject to availability, as these items are limited and offered on a first-come, first-served basis. A reservation fee is required to secure your booking.

To check the availability of a specific gown, kindly send us a photo of your preferred design along with the date of your event so we can assist you properly.

We have various sizes available, including small and medium. Most of our gowns are adjustable to ensure a better fit for different body types.

We can also make adjustments based on your preference, depending on the availability of the necessary materials and equipment.

Our shop is located at Talisay St., Fatima Chapel, Buhangin DC. Landmarks include SPMC, Dumanlas, and Buhangin Gym. You can find us on Google Maps for easy navigation.

Once you send us a message, we will provide you with the exact location and a map guide to our place.

Commuters Landmark & Directions: SPMC, Dumanlas, Buhangin Gym. Sa Buhangin Gym, naog tapos sakay tricycle pa-Talisay. Kung jeep, sakyon ang Buhangin na jeep tapos naog sa Purok Talisay and baktas paubos.
